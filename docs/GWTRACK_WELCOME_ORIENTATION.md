## Welcome

Welcome to **GWTrack** — the private operating portal for Greatway Financial Group teams.

GWTrack is designed so you always know **what to do next**, your CFM and leaders can **see progress clearly**, and the tools you need for prospecting, licensing, training, mentoring, and production live in **one place**.

This orientation walks you through:

1. Why the portal exists  
2. How the layout works  
3. Your dashboard (command center)  
4. Progress trackers and checklists  
5. Learning and resources  
6. Communications and scheduling  
7. Team visibility and mentoring areas  
8. CRM and prospect tools (when enabled for you)  
9. What to do in your first days  
10. Where to get help  

You will only see menu items your **role and permissions** allow. If something described here is missing from your sidebar, it is intentional for your current access level.

## 1. Portal purpose

### What GWTrack is

GWTrack is your **agency operating system**. It connects the work associates do every day into a shared, trackable journey:

| Journey stage | What GWTrack helps with |
|---|---|
| **Join & orient** | Invitation registration, profile setup, onboarding checklist |
| **Get licensed** | Licensing milestones, compliance & renewals |
| **Learn & grow** | Training Center (Academy), assessments, CFM training |
| **Apprentice** | Field Apprenticeship Program (FAP) with CFM guidance |
| **Produce** | Prospects, FNAs, appointments, production posting |
| **Recruit** | Invites, recruiting pipeline, downline visibility |
| **Advance** | Goals, rank advancement, recognition |
| **Lead & mentor** | Team dashboards, CFM Portal, coaching tools |

### What GWTrack is not

- It is **not** a public website. Access requires an invitation (or admin account creation) and an active login.
- It is **not** a replacement for carrier portals or licensing boards — it **tracks** your progress and keeps your team aligned.
- Ranks (FA → EP) are **Greatway advancement levels**. Roles (member, CFM, team-leader, agency-owner, admin, etc.) control **what you can see and do inside GWTrack**.

### Core principles

- **Clear next steps** — Trackers and tasks show what is open, pending confirmation, or due.
- **Hierarchy visibility** — Unless content is marked global, you generally see your own records and your sponsorship downline — not the whole company.
- **Confirmation when it matters** — Many checklist items you mark complete may need a CFM, sponsor, or leader to confirm before they count as finished.
- **One hub for the team** — Resources, announcements, calendar, mentoring, and CRM tools stay connected to the same member profile.

## 2. Finding your way around

After you sign in, you land on **My Dashboard**. The screen has three main areas:

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Top bar** | Global search, notifications bell, account menu |
| **Left sidebar** | Main navigation, grouped by work area |
| **Main content** | The page you are working on |

### Sidebar groups (current portal map)

Expand or collapse a group by clicking its name. Groups you open are remembered in your browser.

| Group | Focus |
|---|---|
| **My Workspace** | Dashboard, profile, tasks, invites, daily activities, messages, calendar, appointments |
| **My Progress** | Onboarding, FAP, licensing, compliance, assessments, rank, CFM training, goals, production |
| **Learning & Resources** | Training Center plus documents, videos, webinars, and links |
| **CRM Funnel System** | Cross-pipeline CRM views (permission-based) |
| **Prospect Management** | Personal sales CRM, recruiting pipeline, FNA, follow-ups |
| **My Team** | Downline dashboard, directs, genealogy, hierarchy, org chart |
| **Mentoring & CFM** | CFM Portal, trainees, effectiveness, mentor scheduling |
| **Communications** | Notifications, support, hub, campaigns, recognition, events |
| **Admin Management** | Setup and administration (leaders / admins only) |

### Global search

Use the search box in the top bar to find team members (when allowed), prospects, documents, videos, training, tasks, calendar events, and announcements. Type at least two characters. Results respect your permissions.

### Notifications

The **bell** shows recent alerts (tasks, mentoring, training, announcements, checklist confirmations, and more). Open **Communications → Notifications** for the full inbox and delivery preferences where enabled.

## 3. My Dashboard — your command center

**Path:** My Workspace → **My Dashboard** (`/dashboard`)

The dashboard greets you by name and rank/team, then surfaces what matters today.

### What you typically see

| Element | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Welcome message** | Confirms your rank and team context |
| **Daily quote** | Short motivation for the day |
| **Profile completion** | Highlights missing required profile fields so your team can support you |
| **Progress trackers** | Percent complete for Onboarding, Licensing, FAP, Training, and Goals (when enabled) |
| **Quick actions** | One-click paths to tasks, prospects, training, messages, calendar, bookings, resources, and more |
| **Quick links** | Curated external / library links by category |

Quick actions adapt to your permissions. Examples include:

- Open **My Tasks**
- **Create My Team** (when eligible)
- Jump into **Prospects** or log **Prospect Activity**
- **Schedule Phone Call**
- Continue **Training**
- Open **Messages**, **Goals**, **Calendar**, **Book Session**, or **Resources**

### How to use the dashboard each day

1. Check **profile completion** if a prompt appears — finish required fields first.  
2. Scan **progress trackers** — click any that lag behind.  
3. Use **quick actions** for the work you will do today.  
4. Review the **notification bell** for confirmations or assignments waiting on you.  

Leaders may also see team-oriented summaries and alerts depending on role.

## 4. Trackers and personal progress

**Path:** Sidebar → **My Progress**

Trackers are checklist- and milestone-driven pages that answer: *Where am I, and what is next?*

### How checklists work

1. Open the tracker for your program (for example **My Onboarding**).  
2. Complete the work described for each item.  
3. Mark the item done in GWTrack.  
4. If the item **requires confirmation**, it moves to a pending state until a notified party (often your CFM, sponsor, or leader) confirms or sends it back.  
5. If confirmation is **not** required, the item can complete automatically when you mark it done.  

Country-specific steps appear only when they match your profile country. Required vs optional steps are labeled on the checklist.

Responsible / notified party codes you may see on items include **Self**, **SP** (sponsor), **AO** (agency owner), **TL** (team leader), **CFM**, and **TR** (trainer).

### Tracker overview

| Tracker | Path | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **My Onboarding** | `/onboarding` | New-member orientation: agreements, setup, first actions |
| **Licensing Tracker** | `/licensing` | Study, exam, application, appointments, and related milestones |
| **Compliance & Renewals** | `/compliance` | Ongoing licenses, E&O, AML, CE, carrier appointments after initial licensing |
| **Field Apprenticeship** | `/field-apprenticeship` | FAP phases completed with your CFM |
| **CFM Training** | `/cfm-training` | Path to Certified Field Mentor certification |
| **Assessments** | `/assessments` | Knowledge checks tied to learning |
| **Rank Advancement** | `/rank-advancement` | Rank ladder, requirements, and evidence |
| **Goals & Performance** | `/goals` | SMART goals, planner, scorecard, coaching views (permission-based) |
| **Production Dashboard** | `/team/production` | Posted production trends; leaders see team rollups |

### Related workspace tools

These live under **My Workspace** but support the same progress story:

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **My Profile** | Contact info, photo, licenses, production entries, invites, checklist snapshot |
| **My Tasks** | List / board / calendar of personal and team tasks |
| **My Invites** | Registration invitation links for new recruits |
| **My Daily Activities** | Activity logging tied to your day-to-day execution |
| **My Calendar** | Personal and shared calendar events |
| **My Appointments** | Booking requests and mentoring / appointment sessions |

### Tip for new associates

In your first weeks, prioritize this sequence:

**Profile → Onboarding → Training Center → Licensing → Tasks from your CFM → Resources**

Your dashboard progress widgets will reflect that same journey.

## 5. Learning and resources

**Path:** Sidebar → **Learning & Resources**

### Training Center (GWTrack Academy)

**Path:** `/training`

Self-paced learning for courses, learning paths, assignments, certifications, and achievements. Leadership or your CFM may assign required courses. Coaching views help mentors monitor trainee learning.

Use Training Center when you need structured lessons; use the Resource Library when you need a document, script, or recording quickly.

### Resource Library

| Area | Path | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Library Home** | `/resources` | Overview of the library |
| **Documents** | `/resources/documents` | PDFs, packets, forms, scripts — preview and download |
| **Videos** | `/resources/videos` | Training and skills videos |
| **Recorded Webinars** | `/resources/recorded-webinars` | Archived live sessions |
| **Links** | `/resources/links` | Quick links, Zoom rooms, external tools |

**Favorites:** Star important documents so they are easy to find again from the library experience.

Resources may be global to the agency or scoped by category. If you cannot open a file, ask your CFM or leader — access can be permission- or role-based.

## 6. Communications

**Path:** Sidebar → **Communications** (plus Messages / Calendar in **My Workspace**)

Staying connected is part of how the team operates — not an optional extra.

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Notifications** | Full alert inbox for tasks, checklists, mentoring, training, and system messages |
| **Help & Support** | Submit a support ticket; browse help content when available |
| **Communication Hub** | Company / agency announcements and featured posts |
| **Leadership Desk** | Leadership publishing and monitoring tools (leaders) |
| **Campaigns** | Multi-step communication campaigns |
| **Recognition** | Celebrate wins on the recognition wall |
| **Events** | Agency event agenda and details |
| **My Messages** | Direct and group conversations with teammates (when enabled) |
| **My Calendar** | Schedule visibility for personal, team, and agency events |
| **My Appointments / Mentor Scheduling** | Book mentoring sessions; mentors manage availability and booking links |

### Communication habits that work

- Check the **bell** at the start and end of your day.  
- Read **Communication Hub** announcements so you do not miss policy or event updates.  
- Use **Messages** for quick coordination; use **tasks** when something needs a due date and owner.  
- Book mentoring time through **Appointments / Mentor Scheduling** instead of ad-hoc only chats when a formal session is needed.

## 8. Prospects, CRM, and production (when enabled)

These areas appear when your role includes prospect / CRM permissions. They are how personal production and recruiting become visible and manageable.

### Prospect Management

**Path:** Prospect Management group

Your **private insurance sales CRM** — prospects belong to you unless shared.

Common tools:

- Summary dashboard and prospect lists  
- **Follow-Up Center** and **Activity Logs**  
- **Sales Pipeline** (insurance funnel kanban)  
- **Appointments**  
- **FNA Management** (financial needs analysis workflows and CFM review queues)  
- **Analytics & Goals**, **AI Coach**, **Import Prospects**  
- **Access Manager** for sharing rules  

### Recruiting Pipeline

Separate from the insurance sales funnel. Use it to move associate candidates from interest through registration, licensing start, and active associate status.

### CRM Funnel System

A permissioned set of funnel views (leads, sales, recruiting, onboarding, FAP, licensing, compliance, contracting, consultant tracker). Use it for broader pipeline visibility when your role includes CRM funnel access.

### Production

Post production from **My Profile**. Review personal and (for leaders) team production on the **Production Dashboard**. Production often feeds goals and leadership visibility.

## 10. Your first-week orientation checklist

Use this as a practical welcome plan. Check items off with your CFM or sponsor.

### Day 1 — Arrive and set up

- [ ] Sign in and complete **email verification** if prompted  
- [ ] Open **My Profile** — photo, phone, timezone, country/jurisdiction, GW Associate ID details as required  
- [ ] Clear **profile completion** items on the dashboard  
- [ ] Find **My Messages** and send a short hello to your sponsor or CFM  
- [ ] Browse **Communication Hub** for current announcements  

### Days 2–3 — Orientation trackers and learning

- [ ] Start **My Onboarding** and mark completed steps  
- [ ] Open **Training Center** and begin assigned or recommended courses  
- [ ] Bookmark key **Documents** (welcome packet, scripts, how-to guides)  
- [ ] Review **My Tasks** for anything assigned to you  

### Days 4–7 — Licensing, calendar, and rhythm

- [ ] Open **Licensing Tracker** and understand your next milestone  
- [ ] Add important dates to **My Calendar**  
- [ ] Learn how **notifications** and checklist **confirmation** work with your CFM  
- [ ] If prospecting is enabled for you, take a guided tour of **Prospect Management** with your mentor  
- [ ] Schedule your next mentoring touchpoint via **Appointments / Mentor Scheduling**  

### Ongoing weekly rhythm

| Cadence | Habit |
|---|---|
| **Daily** | Dashboard → tasks → notifications → one progress tracker push |
| **Several times a week** | Training lessons; prospect/recruiting activity if active |
| **Weekly with CFM** | Review checklist confirmations, FAP/licensing blockers, goals |
| **Ongoing** | Keep profile licenses and compliance records current |

## 11. Visibility and privacy — what others can see

Understanding visibility builds trust and prevents surprises.

- **Your private CRM prospects** stay yours unless you share them.  
- **Downline / mentoring views** let sponsors, CFMs, and leaders see progress appropriate to their role.  
- **Global resources and announcements** may be visible agency-wide.  
- **Admins** can manage users and setup tables; that does not mean every member can see every other member’s CRM.  
- If you believe someone should (or should not) see a record, ask your team leader or agency owner — access is permission-driven.
