## Purpose

This guide provides an overview of Greatway’s promotion structure and compensation framework. It is intended to help agents understand the language, structure, and opportunities involved in building a business with Greatway.

### Promotion Definitions

- **Producer:** Promotions earned through personal production, based on an agent’s individual business performance and consistency in serving clients.
- **Builder:** Promotions earned through team-building, based on an agent’s ability to recruit, develop, and lead others while driving team growth.

## Organizational Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Team | An agent or associate who has recruits, including extended downlines, where none are yet Field Leaders. |
| Field Leader | Agents at the SVP level and above. |
| Baseshop | An SVP or higher who leads a team of agents and associates that are not yet Field Leaders. |
| Superbase | A Field Leader who has first-generation Field Leaders within their downline. |
| Super Team | A Field Leader baseshop team, Superbase team, and up to 6 generations Field Leader team. |
